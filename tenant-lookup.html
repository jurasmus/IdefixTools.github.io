How it works

Every Microsoft Entra ID tenant exposes a public OpenID Connect discovery document at https://login.microsoftonline.com/<tenantId>/v2.0/.well-known/openid-configuration. This tool fetches that document directly from your browser and surfaces the key fields: cloud instance, tenant region, issuer and authority endpoints.

  • Worldwide (Commercial): login.microsoftonline.com
  • US Government (GCC High / DoD): login.microsoftonline.us
  • China (operated by 21Vianet): login.partner.microsoftonline.cn
